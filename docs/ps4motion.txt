Eden / PS4 Controller Motion Controls on Arch

Problem:
Eden could use the DualShock 4 buttons/sticks, but motion setup would hang at “shake controller.”

Diagnosis:
Running:

    testcontroller

showed:

    HIDAPI_SetupDeviceDriver() couldn't open /dev/hidraw8: Permission denied

SDL was falling back to /dev/input/eventXX for normal controls, but could not access the hidraw device needed for gyro/accelerometer data.

The controller was a DualShock 4 Slim:

    VID: 054c
    PID: 09cc

and SDL reported:

    bluetooth 1

So the USB-style udev rule alone was not enough for the Bluetooth hidraw device.

Fix:
Create:

    /etc/udev/rules.d/70-dualshock4.rules

with:

    KERNEL=="hidraw*", ATTRS{idVendor}=="054c", ATTRS{idProduct}=="09cc", MODE="0660", TAG+="uaccess"
    KERNEL=="hidraw*", KERNELS=="*054C:09CC*", MODE="0660", TAG+="uaccess"

Then reload udev:

    sudo udevadm control --reload-rules

Disconnect/reconnect the controller.

Verify with:

    testcontroller

The important part is that this should no longer appear:

    Failed to open /dev/hidrawX: Permission denied

After that, Eden can detect the DS4 gyro when configuring Motion.

Optional:
Instead of maintaining custom rules manually, install the AUR package:

    yay -S game-devices-udev
